# Voice & Tone

> The verbal identity of Sky Frogs: how quests, the CurseForge page, and player-facing text should read. Companion to [branding.md](./branding.md) (visual identity). Settled 2026-05-25.

## Audience: broad / accessible

Write for a player who may be newer to modded Minecraft but isn't a child and isn't dumb. Explain the frog loop and any non-obvious mechanic **once**, clearly, then trust them. Never talk down. A skyblock/modded veteran should find it brisk and never padded; a newcomer should never feel lost.

## Voice: dry, warm, frog-aware

The premise - *frogs replace mining* - is inherently absurd and charming. The voice's job is to play that absurdity **completely straight**, with wry confidence, light swamp/frog texture, and real warmth underneath. Picture a laconic, faintly amused swamp guide who finds nothing odd about iron coming from a frog.

It's a blend, weighted in this order:

1. **Dry wit (primary)** - deadpan, understated, confident. The comedy is in treating the ridiculous as routine. This is what makes it *fun*, and it doesn't fatigue over hundreds of quests.
2. **Frog/swamp texture (seasoning)** - lean into the theme in imagery and the occasional aside. Sparingly.
3. **Warm & clear (the floor)** - every line still plainly says what to do and why it matters. Non-negotiable: the wit rides on top of clarity, never replaces it.

## Do

- Lead with *why you care*, then *what to do*. Momentum first.
- Keep it short. Quest descriptions are 1-4 short lines; players skip walls of text.
- Let the premise carry the humor. Underplay it.
- Vary register by quest weight (see the dial below).

## Don't

- Don't pun every line. Frog puns land once a chapter, grate every quest. At ~750 quests, restraint is survival.
- Don't be cute at the cost of clarity. If the player can't tell what to do, the joke failed.
- Don't pad, lecture, or over-explain. Say it once.
- Don't be sarcastic *at the player*. The wit is about the world, never mocking them.

## Register dial

Match the humor to the moment:

- **Intro / milestone / chapter-cap quests:** personality up. This is where the voice shines.
- **Routine collect/craft/build quests:** mostly functional, one light touch at most. Respect the player's time.

## The phrasing this replaces

The line that kicked this off:

- BAD: "the frog produces a Configurable Froglight **stamped with** that resource" (jargon-y, opaque)
- GOOD: "feed a Resource Slime to its matching frog and it hands back a Froglight **loaded with** that resource."

## Sample - the core loop

> Out here there's no ore to mine and no mineshaft to dig. There are, however, a great many frogs - and it turns out that's the same thing.
>
> Feed a Resource Slime to its matching frog and it hands back a Froglight loaded with that resource. Smelt it for the ingot. The frogs do the mining; you run the swamp.

## Mechanics

- **ASCII punctuation only** - no em/en-dashes (use hyphen, comma, colon, parens). Matches the repo-wide rule.
- **FTB Quests color codes** (`&a`, `&e`, ...) used sparingly to highlight key nouns (species, items), not for decoration.
- **Proper nouns capitalized** consistently: Cave Frog, Resource Slime, Configurable Froglight, Bog Slime Milk.
