# Quest-Text Editorial Audit - Shared Brief (GitHub issue #169)

You are one of ~27 read-only audit agents, each assigned ONE FTB Quests chapter. Your job:
audit every quest in your chapter, ground-truth every mechanic claim against the actual
mod, and write a structured findings file with before/after rewrites. You DO NOT edit the
lang file or chapter files. You ONLY write your own findings file.

## Where things live

- **Quest TEXT** (what you audit): `F:/minecraft-repos/sky-frogs/pack/config/ftbquests/quests/lang/en_us.snbt`
  Flat file keyed by quest ID: `quest.<ID>.title`, `quest.<ID>.quest_subtitle`, `quest.<ID>.quest_desc` (a list of lines).
- **Quest STRUCTURE** (tasks/rewards, to check text-vs-task alignment): `.../quests/chapters/<your_chapter>.snbt`
- **Pack recipe/mechanic OVERRIDES** (check these FIRST): `F:/minecraft-repos/sky-frogs/pack/kubejs/server_scripts/`, `.../kubejs/data/`, `.../config/`
- **Mod jars** (ground truth): `C:/Users/User/curseforge/minecraft/Instances/Sky Frogs/mods/` (155 jars; each is a zip)

## Ground-truth protocol (the #1 priority - accuracy)

For EVERY sentence that describes how a mod mechanic works (a recipe, a machine, a block
behavior, a drop, an item name), VERIFY it. Never trust the existing copy or your own memory.

Order of authority:
1. **Pack override first.** Grep `pack/kubejs/server_scripts/` and `pack/kubejs/data/` for the
   item/recipe. The pack overrides some mod recipes (e.g. quartz-free storage, catalyst recipes,
   dissolution slime recipes). If the pack overrides it, the pack version is the truth.
2. **Then the mod jar.** Jars are zips. Useful commands (run via Bash):
   - List: `unzip -l "<mods>/<jar>" | grep -iE "recipe/.*<thing>"`
   - Recipes (NeoForge 1.21.1): `data/<ns>/recipe/<name>.json` (singular `recipe`)
   - Item/block display names: `assets/<ns>/lang/en_us.json` (grep the id)
   - Tags: `data/<ns>/tags/...`
   - Dump a file: `unzip -p "<mods>/<jar>" "data/<ns>/recipe/<name>.json"`
   - To find the jar for a mod, list the mods dir and match by name.

Recipe JSON `type` tells you the machine/mechanic. Example (the bug that triggered this issue):
JDT `data/justdirethings/recipe/raw_ferricore_ore-goospread.json` is
`type: justdirethings:goospread, input: minecraft:iron_block -> output: raw_ferricore_ore` -
so ferricore comes from goo spreading into an adjacent iron BLOCK, NOT from "seeding soil with a
raw resource" (an invented mechanic the old text described). Recipe JSON is the authority.

## The 8 dimensions to check per quest

1. **Factual accuracy** - every described mechanic matches the mod (ground-truthed, per above).
   This is the top priority. Flag invented mechanics, wrong ingredients, wrong machines, wrong drops.
2. **Task-vs-text alignment** - the prose matches what the chapter's task actually requires/rewards
   (read the task `type`/`item`/`count` in the chapter .snbt).
3. **Terminology** - "Froglight" never "Configurable Froglight" in player text. Block/item names match
   the mod's in-game lang exactly. Correct mod + author names (Just Dire Things is by Direwolf20;
   spell mod names right).
4. **Voice & tone** - THE TARGET: **factual without being technical, helpful without being verbose.**
   The pack serves new AND expert players. State the real mechanic plainly; CUT jargon and mod-internal
   terms (recipe-type ids like "goospread," tick counts, internal tier numbers) unless they change what
   the player does; TRIM padding. Shortest text that lets a new player succeed. Keep the existing warm,
   second-person register and the `&`-color accents - don't flatten the personality, just tighten it.
5. **Formatting** - color/format codes (`&e`,`&a`,`&b`,`&f`,`&9`,`&r`, ...) balanced; every accent run
   closed with `&r`; no stray/unclosed codes; no broken `\n` or quote escaping.
6. **Punctuation policy** - ASCII only. NO em dash, NO en dash. Replace any with `-`, `,`, `:`, `(...)`,
   or restructure. Flag every occurrence (`U+2014`, `U+2013`).
7. **Stale/missing references** - no mention of cut or renamed content; flag content that should be
   covered but isn't (only note what's clearly missing - you only see your own chapter).
8. **Spelling & grammar.**

## GENERATED chapters - do not propose direct lang edits

`whole_pond` and `sister_ponds` are the Completionist census chapters, auto-generated by
`tools/gen_completionist_chapters.py` from PF variant data. Their lang text is emitted by that
generator. If you are assigned one of these: audit normally, but for any text fix, your before/after
must note **"fix belongs in tools/gen_completionist_chapters.py (generated chapter)"** - a direct lang
edit would be wiped on the next regen. Find the offending template string in the generator and cite it.
(If you're unsure whether your chapter is generated, grep the generator for one of your quest IDs/titles.)
