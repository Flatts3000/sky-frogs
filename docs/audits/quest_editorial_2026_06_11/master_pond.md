## Per-quest findings

### The Long Compression (quest.6A0D000000000002)
- Disposition: CLEAN
- Note: factually solid. The compressor also needs an Ultimate Catalyst per singularity (reusable, not consumed - EC guide: "does not get used up"), but the text deliberately keeps it high-level and the JEI recipe shows the catalyst; not worth adding. Count-agnostic phrasing ("a thousand", "every resource") is the right call - it survives PF roster growth.

### Your First Singularity (quest.6A0D000000000005)
- Disposition: CLEAN
- Note: "Quantum Compressor", "Iron Singularity", "a thousand iron" all verified against the jar and pack config. Good.

### The Ultimate Singularity (quest.6A0D000000000008)
- Disposition: EDIT
- Issues: Terminology: the block's in-game name is "Ultimate Crafting Table" (`block.extendedcrafting.ultimate_table`), not "Ultimate Table". The verb chapter is internally named `the_ultimate_table`, but player text should match the in-game block name. Low severity - the mechanic (craft the Ultimate Singularity in that table) is correct.
- BEFORE:
  `"Gather one Singularity for every resource the frogs make - every last one - and crystallize them together in the Ultimate Table into the &dUltimate Singularity&r: the densest form of everything your frogs ever made. This is the proof you automated every last farm."`
- AFTER:
  `"Gather one Singularity for every resource the frogs make - every last one - and crystallize them together in the Ultimate Crafting Table into the &dUltimate Singularity&r: the densest form of everything your frogs ever made. This is the proof you automated every last farm."`

### The Sky Frogs Master Frog (quest.6A0D00000000000B)
- Disposition: EDIT
- Issues: Task-vs-text alignment: the recipe is `FFF/FUF/sFs` - 6 Froglights ring the Ultimate Singularity, but the two bottom corners are Sweetslime (`productivefrogs:sweetslime`), not Froglights. "ring it with Froglights" implies all 8 surrounding slots are froglights; a player following the prose literally will fail the craft (8 froglights leaves no room for the 2 sweetslime). Add the sweetslime.
- BEFORE:
  `"Set the Ultimate Singularity in a crafting table, ring it with Froglights, and craft the &aSky Frogs Master Frog&r. It does nothing. It is everything."`
- AFTER:
  `"Set the Ultimate Singularity in a crafting table, ring it with Froglights, drop a Sweetslime in each bottom corner, and craft the &aSky Frogs Master Frog&r. It does nothing. It is everything."`

### You Out-Frogged the Game (quest.6A0D00000000000E)

- Note: pure send-off flavor, no mechanic claims. Tone fits the endgame. Tiers named correctly map to the six PF categories.
